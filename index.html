🍜 Maggi Recipe

⏱ Preparation

2 Minutes

🔥 Cooking

3 Minutes

🍽 Serves

1 Person

🛒 Ingredients

  • 1 packet Maggi noodles
  • 1½ cups water
  • 1 Maggi tastemaker
  • ½ small onion
  • ½ tomato
  • 1 teaspoon oil
  • Chilli flakes

👨‍🍳 Preparation

  1. Heat a pan and add one teaspoon of oil.
  2. Add chopped onion and tomato. Cook for about a minute.
  3. Pour 1½ cups of water into the pan and bring it to a boil.
  4. Add the Maggi noodles and tastemaker.
  5. Cook for 2–3 minutes while stirring occasionally.
  6. Add chilli flakes and serve hot!
